Local typography for reference-led builds

Unmodified font files from the official Google Fonts repository:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/librecaslondisplay
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/librecaslontext

LibreCaslonDisplay-Regular.ttf is the regular display face.
LibreCaslonText.ttf is the variable LibreCaslonText[wght].ttf file.
Their copyright notices and SIL Open Font License texts are included alongside them.

The generator copies the fonts and license files into website/fonts/ when a
rendered reference uses serif typography. They substitute for proprietary fonts;
reference-site font files, photographs, logos, and branding are not copied.
